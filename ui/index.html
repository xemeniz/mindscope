HOW TO USE

1. Put it last on the master
After the limiter. It only measures: the sound comes out exactly as it goes in.
2. Wave
The track as it plays, split in lows (pink), mids (yellow) and highs (blue), like the CDJ.
3. Spectrum
With 4.5 dB/oct a balanced house mix looks almost flat. Hover to read the frequency, the note and the level. FREEZE stops it.
4. Loudness
Short term while you mix, integrated for the whole song (it starts again when you press play). True peak at -1 dBTP or lower.
5. Stereo
LOW MONO OK means kick and bass are mono below 120 Hz. WIDE LOWS means something down there is in stereo: in a club it can lose punch.
